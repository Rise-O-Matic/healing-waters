What We Do

Restoring dignity,
one shower at a time

More than just showers — we provide a full circle of support to help our guests reclaim their lives.

Hot Showers

Sanitized mobile and on-site showers with clean towels, washcloths, soap, and shampoo. Up to 22 showers per session from our mobile unit.

Clean Clothing

New underwear, socks, and gently-used clothing donations. Seasonal items and toiletries to help guests stay safe and comfortable.

Medical Clinic

Dr. Moses' Wellness Clinic runs every 3rd Friday — free consultation, treatment, and healthcare services for those in need.

Haircuts & Grooming

Volunteer barbers and stylists provide haircuts, helping guests feel confident for job interviews and daily life.

Job Placement

Partnership with the Salvation Army connects guests to job training and employment opportunities for lasting change.

Community Care

Food bags, laundry services, bus tickets, bicycles, prayer circles, and holiday programs including Christmas wish lists for 80-100 guests.

Our Story

Born from compassion,
built by community

Healing Waters, Inc. was established in May 2019 as an all-volunteer organization of compassionate individuals with a shared vision and commitment to the homeless.

Father Dominic Vu, from The City of God, provided the seed money to implement this vision. Freedom in Christ Church was building a mobile shower unit, and HW contracted with them for its use twice a week at locations accessible to the homeless.

In December 2019, HW was incorporated and became a 501(c)(3) non-profit company. Local churches and communities supported HW and provided volunteers. For optimal use, showers were set at locations and times to coincide with hot lunch distribution by other organizations.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide hot showers and other support services to the homeless and indigent, to restore their dignity, to heal their broken spirits, and bring about wholeness and self-worth through love.

Cleansing the body is a basic hygienic need taken for granted by so many, but unavailable to the homeless. We are intentional in hoping that with the grace of God, our services will be a catalyst for positive change and render healing on many levels.
